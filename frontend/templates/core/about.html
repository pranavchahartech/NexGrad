Our Purpose

Bridging the Gap Between Learning and Hiring

Traditional campus recruitment is burdened with inefficiencies. Students spend months mass-applying with static PDF resumes, while recruiters are overwhelmed with thousands of unverified applications with no reliable signal on real-world engineering capability.

Nexgrad flips the paradigm. Instead of relying on keyword heuristics and prestige bias, our platform evaluates candidates through demonstrated code repositories, device-neutral technical tests, and explainable match scoring that highlights exact requirement alignment.
